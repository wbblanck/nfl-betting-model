Model EV (the green pill) is our estimate of the return per $1 staked at the best price shown: the mix probability divided by that price's implied probability (fees included), minus 1. A 55% side at +100 (50% implied) shows 10.0% EV. It is only as good as the mix probability, which has not beaten DraftKings on return, so read it as the model's view, not a measured edge. Where the best price is not DraftKings, the EV at DraftKings is in the chips below the pick.

Market EV (the "Mkt" pill) judges the same price against the other venues instead of our model: the median of their no-vig probabilities, leaving out the venue with the best price, divided by the price's implied probability, minus 1. It falls back to DraftKings' no-vig price when fewer than two other venues quote both sides. It is line shopping, not a forecast, and it is unvalidated: a pre-registered test on 2025 found too few bets to tell (report). Under each pick, every venue's price is listed with its implied % and the dollars behind it; the strip places them on one scale, with the tick at the fair probability.
