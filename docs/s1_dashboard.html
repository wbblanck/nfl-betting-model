What the rating backs this week

For every game the rating picks a side, then finds the best number for that side across five books. The edge is how many points better that number is than the rating's fair line; expected CLV is the part of the edge the line has historically moved toward the rating by kickoff (0.235 × edge). The rating does not beat closing spreads (2022-2025 backtest); its case is closing-line value from opening lines (2008-2024). Labels follow the pre-registered rules; they are tracker records, not recommendations.
