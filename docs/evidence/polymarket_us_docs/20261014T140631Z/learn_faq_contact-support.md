# How do I contact support?

> How to reach Polymarket US support

## In-app chat

You can contact support through the in-app chat in your account settings.

## Email support

You can reach us at [**support@polymarket.us**](mailto:support@polymarket.us)

## What to include

For faster responses, include:

* What you were trying to do
* Error messages
* Reference IDs
* Screenshots or screen recordings
