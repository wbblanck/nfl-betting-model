# How are Polymarket odds determined?

Polymarket odds come entirely from user trades. Prices update whenever a buy or sell order is matched in the central order book, and those trades reflect what users believe right now. As new information appears, traders adjust their orders, and the odds move in real time to capture the current market sentiment. Polymarket US does not set prices or take the other side of your trades.
