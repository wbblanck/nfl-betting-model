# Why can't I withdraw my funds?

In most cases, your deposit is still processing. Deposits must fully clear before they can be withdrawn, which typically takes **3–4 business days**. Business days do not include weekends or holidays.

When you deposit, we may credit some or all of it as instant buying power so you can trade right away. Instant buying power has a cap, so part of a larger deposit may only become available once it clears. To withdraw the original deposit or any proceeds from instant buying power, the funds must fully clear.

If your deposit is still pending after **5 business days**, contact support.
