# Browse Markets

> Learn how to browse active market categories on Polymarket

Polymarket US offers markets on sports (NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, MLS, college sports, tennis, golf, and more), politics, crypto, economics, weather, tech, and culture.

## How to Browse

Find active markets on your home screen. Scroll across the top section to view the available categories.

Each market shows:

* The **market question**
* Current **Yes and No prices**
* The **resolution date**
