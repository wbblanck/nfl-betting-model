## Reading
Both pre-registered tests fail. Bets on 1.5+ point moves where the rating agreed covered 51.1% at −110 on
2008-2021 (ROI −2.5%) and 45.9% at bet365's closing prices on 2022-2024 (ROI −12.0%). The rating's agreement did not
lift results above following the move alone in a consistent way (2008-2021: 51.1% vs 50.5%; 2022-2024: 45.9% vs
47.9%). Once the line has moved, the moved number has already priced the information. Per the pre-registration this
ends the hybrid at sportsbook closing prices; nothing here is a reason to fade it (that would be a new hypothesis).
